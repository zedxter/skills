Want to know the secret to scaling an engineering team?

Two years ago we were 3 engineers. Today we are 40.

But here's what nobody tells you about growing a team: it's not about hiring. It's about writing things down.

When Acme Bank signed their $2.4M contract with us in May, we had to triple in six months. The only reason it worked: every decision went into a short written doc that new people could read on day one.

We also changed our on-call rotation, moved to a monorepo, and started a weekly demo. All of it helped, in different ways.

I wrote up our doc template here: https://example.com/decision-docs
