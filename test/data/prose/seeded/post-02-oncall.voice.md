# Voice profile: the author

Built from five LinkedIn posts, 2025–2026.

- Short, dry sentences. Mostly 8–15 words. Understatement over excitement.
- Never uses exclamation marks. Never uses emoji. Never says "journey", "thrilled", "incredible", "grateful".
- Opens with a number or a plain fact.
- Credits people by name only with their permission, and never mentions health or personal matters.
- Ends with the practical takeaway or a link in the first comment. No calls to comment.
- Example lines: "We deleted 40 alerts. Nobody noticed." / "The fix was boring. That's the point."
