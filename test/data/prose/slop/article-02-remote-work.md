# Lessons From Three Years of Remote Engineering

Remote work has fundamentally transformed the way engineering teams collaborate. What started as a temporary measure has become a permanent fixture of the modern workplace.

When it comes to remote teams, communication is everything. Without the hallway conversations of the office, teams must be intentional about how they share information, make decisions, and build relationships.

It's not about the tools. It's about the habits.

Asynchronous communication serves as the backbone of a healthy remote culture. Written updates, thoughtful documentation, and clear ownership empower engineers to do their best work without constant interruptions.

But here's the catch: async only works when trust is in place. Teams that micromanage end up with more meetings, not fewer.

The most successful teams I've seen share a few traits. They write things down. They default to transparency. They celebrate wins, big and small.

Ultimately, remote work is not a challenge to overcome but an opportunity to embrace. The teams that thrive will be the ones that adapt, iterate, and grow together.
