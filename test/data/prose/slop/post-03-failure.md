Have you ever shipped a bug to production on a Friday?

I have. And it taught me more than any success ever did.

Last year, a single missing null check took down our checkout flow for two hours. Customers couldn't pay. Support tickets piled up. My stomach dropped.

But here's the thing: the outage wasn't the failure. The failure would have been not learning from it.

We ran a blameless postmortem. We added tests. We improved our alerts. And we built a culture where it's safe to talk about mistakes.

Failure isn't the opposite of success. It's part of it.
