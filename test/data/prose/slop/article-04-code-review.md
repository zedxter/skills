# The Art of the Code Review

Code reviews are one of the most powerful tools in a software team's arsenal. Yet they are often misunderstood, rushed, or treated as a mere formality.

A great code review is not about finding fault. It's about sharing knowledge, improving quality, and building a stronger team.

## Be Kind

The tone of a review matters just as much as its content. A thoughtful comment can turn a frustrating moment into a learning opportunity, while a harsh one can erode trust.

## Be Specific

Vague feedback like "this could be better" leaves authors guessing. Instead, point to the exact line, explain the concern, and suggest an alternative.

## Be Timely

Reviews that sit for days slow everyone down. A quick turnaround keeps momentum high and context fresh.

It's worth noting that the best reviewers also know when to approve. Perfect is the enemy of good, and not every nit needs to block a merge.

At the end of the day, code review is a conversation. When teams approach it with empathy, curiosity, and respect, everyone wins.
