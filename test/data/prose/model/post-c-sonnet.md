We just open-sourced our internal feature-flag library. 🚀

For years it's been the quiet backbone of how our team ships. It let us roll out changes gradually, test in production safely, and turn off a bad release in seconds instead of scrambling for a rollback. Today, it's available to everyone.

Why we built it:
→ Off-the-shelf tools didn't fit our performance and reliability needs
→ We wanted flag evaluation to be fast, predictable, and easy to reason about
→ We wanted flags to be simple for engineers to add, and just as simple to clean up

Why we're sharing it:
Much of what we know about shipping safely, we learned from the open-source community. This is our chance to give something back. We'd also love to see how others use it and make it better.

What you'll find in the repo:
• A lightweight core with minimal dependencies
• Support for percentage rollouts and targeting rules
• Clear docs and examples to get started in minutes

A big thank you to everyone on the team who spent countless hours turning an internal tool into something ready for the world. 👏

The repo is linked in the comments. Try it out, open an issue, send a PR, or tell us what's missing. We're listening.
